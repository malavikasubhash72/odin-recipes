Carrot Halwa

Carrot Halwa, also known as Gajar ka Halwa, is a classic Indian dessert made with grated carrots, milk, ghee, sugar, and aromatic cardamom. It’s slow-cooked until rich and creamy, then garnished with nuts for a delightful crunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg carrots (grated)
  • 1 liter full-fat milk
  • 4–5 tbsp ghee (clarified butter)
  • 1 cup sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 8–10 cashews
  • 8–10 almonds
  • 10–12 pistachios
  • 4–5 green cardamom pods (powdered)
  • 2 tbsp raisins

Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and grate the carrots.
  2. Heat milk in a heavy-bottomed pan and add the grated carrots.
  3. Cook on medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the milk reduces and the carrots soften.
  4. Add ghee and continue cooking until the mixture thickens.
  5. Stir in sugar and cardamom powder; cook until the halwa turns glossy.
  6. In a separate pan, lightly fry cashews, almonds, pistachios, and raisins in ghee.
  7. Mix the fried nuts and raisins into the halwa.
  8. Serve warm, garnished with extra nuts if desired.
