Kunafa

Kunafa (also spelled Knafeh) is a beloved Middle Eastern dessert known for its crispy golden pastry layers and gooey cheese or cream filling. It’s often soaked in fragrant sugar syrup and topped with crushed pistachios, making it both indulgent and aromatic.

Ingredients

  • 500 g shredded kataifi pastry (kunafa dough)
  • 250 g unsalted butter, melted
  • 400 g mozzarella or akkawi cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup milk or cream (optional for richness)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp rose water or orange blossom water
  • 1/2 cup crushed pistachios

Steps

  1. Prepare syrupIn a saucepan, combine sugar and water. Boil until slightly thickened, then add rose water or orange blossom water. Set aside to cool.
  2. Butter pastryMix shredded kataifi pastry with melted butter until evenly coated.
  3. Layer pastrySpread half of the buttered pastry in a greased baking pan.
  4. Add fillingSpread shredded cheese (and cream if using) evenly over the pastry layer.
  5. Top layerCover with the remaining pastry, pressing gently to flatten.
  6. BakeBake at 180°C (350°F) for 35–40 minutes until golden and crisp.
  7. Add syrupPour cooled sugar syrup over hot kunafa immediately after baking.
  8. GarnishSprinkle with crushed pistachios before serving.
