Cake

Cake is a sweetened dessert made primarily from flour,sugar,eggs and fat.It is a staple centerpiece for celebrations worldwide.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup whole milk

Steps

  1. Preheat ovenPreheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Mix dry ingredientsIn a bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Cream butter and sugarBeat butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Add eggs and vanillaAdd eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Stir in vanilla extract.
  5. Combine wet and dryGradually add dry ingredients alternately with milk, beginning and ending with dry ingredients.
  6. Cool and frostCool cakes in pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to wire racks. Frost with buttercream or whipped cream once cooled
